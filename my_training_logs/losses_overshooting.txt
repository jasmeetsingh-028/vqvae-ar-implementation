Issue: Issue while passing z_q and z_q_st {claude suggested}
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

z_q_st is required for gradients to flow through while back prop so decoder gets z_q_st, as it lets gradients flow through it.

z_q on the other hand is used for loss computation but does not allow gradients to flow through it.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Understanding z_q_st or straight through estimator trick:
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

z_q_st = z_e + (z_q - z_e).detach()

During forward pass z_q_st does nothing at all as it works only on the gradients

so the equation becomes: z_q_st = z_e + z_q - z_e = z_q, so z_q during forward pass

But during backward pass: (z_q - z_e).detach() has no gradient so gradient is calculate through z__q_st = z_e
